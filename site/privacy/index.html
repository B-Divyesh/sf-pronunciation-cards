Privacy policy

Privacy

Effective 6 September 2026

Pronunciation Cards does not require an account. Cards and speech previews use local browser features.

Glossary data

Your terms, aliases, IPA, notes, voice choice, and speech rate use browser extension storage on your device.

Matching selected text and creating speech previews run through browser APIs on your device.

Extension permissions

  • Storage stores your glossary and voice settings.
  • Context menus adds commands for selected text.
  • Active tab and scripting access a page only after your toolbar or context-menu action.

Exports and deletion

Exported JSON is a file you control. Delete cards, replace a glossary during import, or remove the extension and its data.

This website

The website has no analytics, cookies, third-party fonts, or third-party scripts. Hosting may keep ordinary security logs.

Questions

Open an issue in the public source repository. Do not include private glossary data in a public issue.
