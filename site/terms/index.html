What the product does

The extension applies user-written aliases when previewing speech or reading selected text through documented browser speech capabilities. It can also create copyable SSML. Speech voices and SSML support differ by browser, operating system, and reading tool.

Pronunciation Cards does not modify an independent screen reader’s internal dictionary and cannot guarantee a pronunciation in every tool. Verify important names and instructions in the reading surface where they will be used.
